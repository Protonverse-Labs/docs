## Not published

PCB layout source files, Gerbers, NC drill files, pick-and-place data, panelization files and the production BOM are intentionally not published. The repository is meant to let you understand the board, verify its wiring, source the same parts and build on the design, not to provide a ready-to-clone manufacturing package.
