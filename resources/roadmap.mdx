This roadmap reflects real, current work: no speculative feature list and no dates that have not been committed to. [Project status](/resources/project-status) says what is verified today.

## Available now

- Working Arduino firmware: camera capture and streaming, Wi-Fi, IMU, secure element, I/O expander, microphone, microSD, RGB and 1 W LED control, and a serial command interface
- Public hardware documentation: overview, pinout, camera guide, firmware guide and flashing guide
- Example sketches for camera capture, Wi-Fi streaming and LED control
- The approved schematic (PDF) and a parts-level BOM
- Real hardware photos
- Firmware under MIT and hardware documentation under CERN-OHL-P v2

## In progress

- A recorded hardware demo: boot, camera, stream
- Crowd Supply campaign preparation. The [pre-launch page](https://www.crowdsupply.com/protonverse-labs/proton-ai-core-vision-plus) is live

## Planned

- Additional firmware examples, as they are extracted and verified from working code
- Expanded troubleshooting and FAQ content as real user issues come in
- Community example submissions through pull requests

<Note>
  This roadmap intentionally does not include unannounced hardware features, on-device AI capabilities or fixed ship dates. If something is not listed here, it has not been committed to.
</Note>
